About Us

'li'tn.' – meaning small in Swedish.

Set up by [Founder Name] and [Founder Name], Liten opened its doors in South Hampstead in 2022 with a simple idea: proper coffee and a bit of community spirit, in a space small enough to feel like your local.

We serve Origin Coffee Roasters as our house blend, alongside a rotating selection of guest beans, specialty teas, and ceremonial-grade matcha — plus a daily-changing pastry counter stocked from local bakeries.

Our sister cafe, Dawn Seal, is just down the road in Maida Vale. It's where we run our Sunday Chess Club and other community events — pop by and say hello.
